How to play

You’re Maxwell’s demon. Get each ball into the chamber that matches its color.

  • Work the gatesTap a demon to open its gate. Tap again once the doorway is clear to close it.
  • Flick a ballDrag and release to send it in a new direction. You get one flick every 10 seconds of play.
  • Seal the chambersWhen every ball is home, close every gate to stop the clock.
Keyboard controls

1–9 toggle gates. Or Tab to a gate and press Enter. Tab to a ball and use an arrow key to flick. Use the Pause button or press Space on the board.
